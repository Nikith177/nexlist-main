What is Nexlist?

Nexlist is a campus marketplace built for students. It allows students within the same college to buy, sell, and rent items easily.

Nexlist is designed specifically for college students who want a simple way to buy, sell, rent items, or offer services within their campus community.

Students can also offer services such as tutoring, repairs, and other campus-based help.

Nexlist focuses on local, trusted interactions within college campuses, making transactions faster and safer.

What can you do on Nexlist?

  • Buy items from other students
  • Sell used or new items
  • Rent products like books, bags, and electronics
  • Offer services to other students

Nexlist is designed to be simple, fast, and student-focused.

Nexlist helps students easily buy, sell, rent, and offer services within their college campus.
